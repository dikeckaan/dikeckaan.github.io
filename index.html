About Me

I'm a passionate software developer and engineer from Istanbul with a deep love for music and technology.

Most of my work lives close to the hardware. I build mu300-linux, which runs a full Ubuntu or OpenWrt next to Android on the ZTE F50, MU300 and U30 Air 5G hotspots, along with the flashing toolkit, VPN module and a family of Magisk modules for those devices. I also write native macOS apps such as easy-vphone, Hackintosh EFIs, a Forza Horizon telemetry dashboard and small Raspberry Pi and Kindle projects.

Almost everything I make is open source. From elegant code to seamless user experiences, I bring ideas to life through thoughtful engineering and design.
